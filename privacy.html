Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you purchase or use the YouTube Automation Starter System, and how it's used.

1. Information we collect

  • Purchase information: the email address you provide when purchasing, used to send you dashboard access.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Paystack. We do not receive or store your card number, bank details, or other sensitive payment information, that is handled entirely by Paystack under its own privacy and security policies.
  • Course progress: the learner dashboard saves your module completion, checklist answers, and worksheet entries so your progress persists between visits. This is stored in your browser (or, in some contexts, via the platform hosting the dashboard) and is not transmitted to us unless you contact us directly.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver access to the course after payment.
  • To respond to support questions you send to our contact email.
  • To send you important updates about the course you purchased, such as content corrections.

We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Third-party services

Payments are processed by Paystack. Course videos, where included, may link to or embed content from YouTube. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing information you provide directly to them.

4. Data retention

We retain your email and purchase record for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support and comply with our own record-keeping obligations.

5. Your rights

You can request details of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us at the address below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

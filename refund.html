Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The YouTube Automation Starter System is a digital product delivered instantly upon payment confirmation. Because of this, our refund policy is intentionally narrow.

1. General policy: no refunds

Once access to the learner dashboard has been delivered to you, the purchase is final. We do not offer refunds for change of mind, not completing the course, or dissatisfaction with your own results, since results depend on factors like your effort, consistency, and decisions made by YouTube that are outside our control.

2. Exceptions

We will provide a refund in the following limited situations:

  • Duplicate payment: you were accidentally charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • Non-delivery: your payment was confirmed but you did not receive access to the dashboard within a reasonable time, and we were unable to resolve this after being contacted.
  • Verified payment error: a technical error on our end resulted in an incorrect charge.

3. How to request a refund

If you believe your situation falls under one of the exceptions above, email us at the address below with your payment reference and a description of the issue. We'll review and respond as quickly as we can.

4. Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Paystack. Processing times beyond that point depend on Paystack and your bank, and are outside our control.
